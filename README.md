Green-Ops: Making AI Sustainable

"Training a single AI model can emit as much carbon as five cars in their lifetime."

Green-Ops is a suite designed to audit and optimize the environmental cost of Artificial Intelligence. By making energy "visible," we empower developers to build lighter, faster, and greener models.

How it Works
1. **Profile:** Attach the tracker to your model.
2. **Analyze:** Visualize the Pareto Front (Accuracy vs. Energy).
3. **Optimize:** Export reports to guide model pruning and quantization.
